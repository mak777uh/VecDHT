re-encoding byte formats (`docs/VecDHT-SPEC-v8.md` §5.1). Each row below is the crate, what it holds,

## What is different from v7.5

Short form; the details and the measurements are in the spec (appendix "what was removed from v7.5 and
why").

* routing: the SimHash prefix is replaced by the coarse quantizer shipped in the artifact, and there
  are exactly 64 shards;
* identity: `PeerId` stays whatever libp2p derives; the role comes from the signed `NodeCert`;
* index: PQ is removed, an int8 vector is stored, and recall stops being a function of quantization;
* PoW: Argon2id is removed, blake3 with the nonce bound to `(owner, shard, epoch, slot, expiry)`;
  the difficulty was re-shot by measurement, not by the invented constant 200 MHash/s (`E-0081`);
* determinism: a fixed point Δ=1/127 instead of a promise of bit-identical float inference —
  sensitivity measured: perturbing one coordinate by 1e-5 changes 0.17 of 384 coordinates and
  `cluster_id` does not change in any of 2000 records under a uniform shift;
* privacy: the wording was honestly weakened — an index node reads what it holds.
